01 — The problem

Drug interactions are common, and easy to miss

30%

of adverse drug events are caused by drug-drug interactions.

2+

drugs is often all it takes to create a risk neither drug carries on its own.

1

reference source (existing interaction databases) that's built for professionals, not the people taking the drugs.

02 — The solution

Enter two or more drugs, get a clear risk reading

  • CHECK Whether the drugs interact at all
  • GRADE Severity, graded Mild / Moderate / Severe with a green / yellow / red marker
  • EXPLAIN Predicted side effects, in plain language rather than clinical shorthand
  • ADVISE A clinical recommendation, including safer alternatives where they exist

03 — How it works

From input to recommendation

1

Input

The user enters two or more drug names.

2

Data

The system draws on DrugBank and FDA Adverse Event Reports for known interaction and side-effect data.

3

AI engine

A machine-learning classification model, paired with NLP over drug labels, scores the interaction and predicts likely effects.

4

Output

An interaction report is returned with a color-coded risk grade and a plain-language explanation.
